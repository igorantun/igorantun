# Minimal portfolio references

Researched 2026-09-16. Igor subsequently identified references 1–4 as his favorites. The [working redesign plan](../redesign-plan.md) captures the proposed direction.

Brief: remake the website from the ground up with a highly minimalist, professional approach, subtle colors and animations, mostly text, intuitive and tidy. Baseline: [Alex Brinza](https://alexbrinza.com/), visually inspected live: small left-aligned text column, pale background, gray secondary text, career chronology, substantial empty space, blurred entrance resolving to readable text.

All 26 candidates below were opened on their primary websites and visually inspected in a desktop browser. Notes are design judgments about the inspected pages, not exhaustive interaction audits. Some sites initially showed entrance transitions; stable views were checked where needed. Theme, viewport, and future redesigns can change their appearance. No claim that every site's motion is subtle or that all are equally close matches. Each first-party link is its row's source.

User feedback: Igor loves Emil, Benji, Paco, and Gavin, describing them as very close to what he has in mind. Their shared restraint is the starting point; exact typography, spacing, palette, and motion remain design choices to review against Igor's much shorter content.

Excluded from close-match recommendations after live inspection: current [Rauno Freiberg](https://rauno.me/) uses oversized type, horizontal panels and a vivid yellow element; [Bryan Haggerty](https://bryanhaggerty.com/) uses saturated purple and a decorative blob; [Andy Matuschak](https://andymatuschak.org/) uses a more complex multi-column research layout and stronger color. Their current appearances should not be described using old minimalist screenshots.
